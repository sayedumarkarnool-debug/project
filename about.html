Our Mission

Celebrating Every Fandom, Everywhere

Founded in 2004 by Jimmy Wales and Angela Beesley, Fandom began as Wikia — a free wiki hosting service. Today it's the definitive destination for fan-created knowledge, bringing together millions of passionate enthusiasts across games, movies, TV, anime, books, and beyond.

We believe every story deserves to be documented, every character deserves a page, and every fan community deserves a home.

🌍
Community

Built by Fans, for Fans

Every article, every edit, and every wiki on Fandom is created and maintained by volunteer contributors — fans just like you. Our community of editors spans the globe, working together to create the most comprehensive entertainment knowledge base ever assembled.

Whether you're a casual reader or a dedicated editor, there's a place for you at Fandom.
